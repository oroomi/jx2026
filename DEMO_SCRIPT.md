# Demo script

Gavel expo voting is pairwise: "better or worse than the last table?" The first ten seconds decide it.
Partners pick their own track winner against their own criterion -- lead with the partner's words for partner judges.

---

## 90-second script (drive and narrate)
| Time | Driver does | Narrator says |
|---|---|---|
| 0-10 s | Feed `/` already loaded: companies surfaced in the last 90 days | The opener sentence. These are the companies your pipeline has not seen yet. |
| 10-30 s | Tap the top row, source chip opens the register record | Every line is a public filing with a link. |
| 30-60 s | Type one of THEIR cohort companies, the four-question page appears | Your four questions, in your order, ten seconds, rules printed under each level. |
| 60-80 s | `/radar`: earliness chart, point at a blank | We would have flagged these N months before Nordic Blue; blanks stay blank. |
| 80-90 s | Swap the thesis file to health-tech Turku | Same code, different file: transferable. Takeaway line. |

If the moment needs data we do not have, change the moment, not the data.

---

## First ten seconds (the hook, all versions share it)

**0-4 s:** one sentence -- "We [do X] for [named user] so that [outcome]." Use the partner's own words. No team
introduction, no market size. Never open with "Hi, we are team X and we built...".

**4-10 s:** the demo moment starts immediately. The map reacts, the answer appears, the voice command lands. The
judge sees something working before they hear any architecture.

## 60-second table version (Gavel expo, crowded hall)

| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0-4 s | One-sentence opener (partner's words) |
| 4-10 s | First visible action on the live URL |
| 10-50 s | Core flow: one drives, one narrates; point at source rows, never the terminal |
| 50-60 s | Takeaway line -- one sentence the judge can repeat at the next table |

Opener (partner words): "We find promising blue-economy startups as early as possible for accelerator and fund teams, so that you see the companies your pipeline has not seen yet, with the public record behind every line."
Takeaway line: "Ocean Opportunity Radar finds blue-economy startups from public records before they reach your pipeline, and answers your four questions with a link under every fact."
Closing number (fill from EVAL.md only when measured): "<N> of 19 cohort companies had a public signal <median> months before they entered Nordic Blue. Scan this and try it on your own company."

## Mid-demo restart line

If a judge arrives while the demo is running, stop immediately:
"Let me start from the top -- [one-sentence opener]."
Jump straight to the first visible action. Never finish the flow you were in.

---

## Three hard questions with answer slots

**1. Who exactly is it for?**
Answer: the accelerator lead (A'Pelago screens 160 applications for 21 places) and the VC associate (Redstone Blue, pre-seed and seed). Monday decision: which five do I call this week, and about what.

**2. What was built this weekend versus pre-existing?**
Answer: see DISCLOSURE.md. Short version: "<the one-line statement from DISCLOSURE.md>". No hedging.

**3. What breaks first at scale?**
Answer: <name one real weakness from ARCHITECTURE.md "What breaks first at scale" section>. Name it before they do.

---

## Closing line (peak-end, JUDGE_LEVERS A6)

The last thing said is ONE real number (with a receipt in EVAL) and the ask. The ask is "scan this and try it on your phone". It is never "vote for us".
Closing line: "<number> <unit>, <what it means for the named user>. Scan this and try it yourself."
Opener languages: English first; add a Finnish or Swedish opener with an English gloss for Madeleine. Ask the judge's preference, do not assume.
Short version (45 s, crowded hall or late rotation): opener, one action, closing line.

---

## Table checklist

- Laptop on the live URL, phone on the live URL, QR card, one-page PILOT printed x10.
- One person at the table at all times during the expo window. Rotate every 30-40 minutes.
- Battery, charger, hotspot. Demo must work on 4G if venue wifi dies.
- 60-second screen recording of the demo path saved on the phone as the fallback.
- Typed fallback ready for any voice input in case the hall is too loud.
- Judge pass (hack-verify): QR card printed and standing next to the laptop, brightness 100 percent, laptop on power, handout on the table, narrator on the aisle side.
- Never say "it normally works" -- switch to the fallback recording instead.
