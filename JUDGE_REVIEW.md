## 3. The ten worst things (ranked by cost in a pairwise vote)

1. **Backtest headline is not supported by the radar's own rule.** `/backtest`: "We would have flagged 5 of 19 before January 2026". The five are found only by their register entry date, with no thesis test. Their registered industry codes are: Sub Marine Store Oy 46900 "Non-specialised wholesale trade", Taikoa Oy 70200 "Business and other management consultancy activities", Finnish Sauna Shop Oy 47279 "Retail sale of other food n.e.c.", Emission-Eye Oy 62100 "Computer programming activities" (I also confirmed 62100 and 2025-06-13 in the PRH API), Sireness Oy 72100 "Research and experimental development on natural sciences and engineering". None of these codes is in `config/thesis.yaml` (it lists 72190, not 72100, and no 46900, 70200, 47279 or 62100), and only Sub Marine Store has a thesis word in its name. The feed's own rule ("A thesis word in the legal name alone never admits a company to the main list") would not have listed them. Fix: in the backtest count a signal only if the company passes the same `store.explain` test as the feed, or relabel the headline "had a public record (not necessarily a flag)".
2. **"Why" lines claim words the linked record does not contain as the thesis means them.** `/` row Skyfora Oy: "Why: The eu project text contains 'hav'." The row text is a GNSS weather-forecast project; the CORDIS record https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101331305 contains "have increased" and "have proven". 'hav' is the Swedish stem for sea. Same pattern: Elmery Oy (hydrometallurgy of metals, https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101315004, "have expressed interest"), AWAKE.AI Oy "contains 'sea'" (https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101318856, "seamless B2B"), R3FINED International Oy Ltd. "contains 'aqua'" (food sensors, record says "aquaculture" only as an adjacent chain), Intellisys Oy "contains 'blue'" (https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101304754, "regional blueprints"), Tapio Palvelut Oy "contains 'blue'" (peatland digital twins). These are the second row to the sixth row of the start page. Fix: match whole words or whole stems, and drop 'hav', 'sea' and 'blue' as stand-alone prefix matches in project text.
3. **Questions 03 and 04 are empty.** `/c/3543918-7` and `/c/2902007-2`: "03 Who comes next: not yet scored: matching rules land Saturday" and "04 What should happen next: not yet scored: next-step rules land Saturday". Fix: merge and deploy matching and next-step before the desk check, or hide the sections until they have content.
4. **The feed is mostly low-confidence register rows.** `/`: "By confidence: high 1, medium 59, low 34", and rows such as Maatz rakennnus & kumppanit Oy (a construction name) shown with "Why: Industry code 39000 ... is on the thesis list." and "How sure: low: keyword or code match only". The footer admits a code is "a hint, not proof". Fix: put medium and high rows first and move code-only rows under the closed "weak" list.
5. **The partner's own organisation appears as a discovered startup.** `/` row "A'Pelago Experience Ab", EU project 2026-09-01, "Matched on project title: 'blue'. BMB2030: BlueMissionBANOS2030". Showing the host as a "new startup" costs credibility instantly. Fix: exclude names containing the partner and portfolio names, and organisations that are not startups (also Solar Foods Oyj, a listed company, appears as "New this quarter").
6. **Business Finland numbers cannot be checked.** `/c/2902007-2` (Kelluu): "Business Finland funding granted in 2026 (year only; the export has no decision date): grant EUR 501 369. Row from the manual dashboard export, no per-row link." The link goes to the generic dashboard page (https://tietopankki.businessfinland.fi/anonymous/extensions/MyonnettyRahoitus/MyonnettyRahoitus.html, a 10 KB app shell), so a judge cannot find the row. It is honest, but it still reads as "high confidence" with a source chip. Fix: say "unverifiable by link, manual export dated 2026-10-16" in the confidence label instead of "high confidence".
7. **Health-tech switch keeps blue-economy wording and lists clinics.** `/?thesis=health_tech_turku`: heading still "Early signals of blue-economy startups, for fund and accelerator teams."; rows such as Forte Terveyspalvelut Oy, "Why: The registered line of business 'Medical specialists activities' contains 'medical'." Fix: heading from the thesis file, and exclude health-practice business lines in the health thesis.
8. **The unknown-name message points at the wrong fix and then shows the whole feed.** `/search?q=Zorbo%20Quantum`: "No public record found for this name yet. Try the registered company name (ending in Oy)." Fine for Finnish firms, wrong for a Danish or Norwegian cohort name, and the full 94-row feed follows. Fix: "Not found in PRH or the declared list. Companies outside Finland are only covered if they are in the declared cohort."
9. **Source table shows zeros that look broken.** `/method` Sources: "BlueInvest pipeline 0 no data", "declared list 0 no data", "prh_notices 0 no data" while the Nordic Blue page uses BlueInvest TRLs. Fix: show "profiles: 19, no dated events" for BlueInvest and hide adapters that are not wired.
10. **Garbled and internal wording on a judged page.** `/method`, Confidence rule: "The only way an source writes. One event shape: company, kind of record, date, source_url, confidence." (a docstring pasted into the page), and Declared datasets lists `RECON_DATA_SOURCES.md` and `RECON_ECOSYSTEM_README.md`. Fix: write one clean sentence and list only data files.

Also noted: Cohort pages for companies outside Finland (for example `/c/no-tunisea`) are almost all "not in public records", which is honest but makes 13 of 19 backtest rows blank; the page says so ("says nothing ab...") in the Limits paragraph.

A judge would say: "They checked themselves against our cohort and showed where they fail." (Valid once finding 1 is fixed.)
