Sonar: distance from the centre is how early we saw them

The sweep is empty where it matters. Not one of the 19 cohort companies sits out in the rings as a company the rule would have found before the programme began, and 11 hold no public record at all.

The centre is cohort entry, 2026-01-01, the programme start month declared by the team, not a published entry date. A blip's distance out is the whole months between the signal it stands for and that date. A filled blip is the signal the thesis rule counts; an outline is a stored record the rule does not admit. Where the rule counts a later signal, the blip sits on that signal, not on an earlier rejected record.
