Read CLAUDE.md, radar/emit.py, radar/adapters/businessfinland.py (year-only date handling and the matching helpers), radar/adapters/cordis.py (how discovery companies are added), config/thesis.yaml, DISCLOSURE.md, research/CREATIVE_SIGNALS.md sections 3 and 7.

Why: the EU fisheries and maritime fund (EMFAF, in Finland EMKVR, Euroopan meri-, kalatalous- ja vesiviljelyrahasto) is blue-economy money by definition, and EU rules oblige Finland to publish the list of beneficiaries with the operation name, amount and dates. Nobody joins that list to young companies. The EU-level reference file has no beneficiaries (verified 16 Oct), so the national list is the source.

Build radar/adapters/emkvr.py:
1. Find the current Finnish EMKVR 2021-2027 beneficiary list (tuensaajaluettelo) on merijakalatalous.fi or the ministry's (MMM) pages; it is usually an Excel or CSV. Also look for the 2014-2020 EMKR list for history. Record the exact URLs and the download date in the adapter docstring and DISCLOSURE.md. Download to data/cache/emkvr/ (gitignored), never commit the file. If no machine-readable list exists, write BLOCKED with what you found and stop.
2. Parse columns honestly (beneficiary name, Y-tunnus if present, operation or project name, measure or priority, amounts granted: EU, national, total; dates: decision or approval date, start, end; municipality). Dates: use day precision when the file has a day, otherwise year precision with the precision field as businessfinland.py does. Never invent a day.
3. Emit "eu_grant" signals (the type exists in emit.SIGNAL_TYPES on main after jx22; if your checkout lacks it, add that one line) with programme "EMKVR", amount, operation name in detail, source_url = the published list URL (plus a row identifier in detail). Match beneficiaries to companies in the store by Y-tunnus first, then folded legal name; unmatched limited companies (Oy, Ab, Oyj) whose operation text or measure matches a thesis keyword (whole word) enter as discovery companies with confidence low, as cordis.py does; public bodies, associations and sole traders are skipped and counted.
4. Run against the committed snapshot (`RADAR_DATA_DIR=data/snapshot`) and report: rows parsed, beneficiaries by type, matched companies, discovery companies, signals by year, total EUR, and the 5 youngest matched companies with dates.
5. Tests on a 20-row synthetic fixture ("Fixture Oy"): parsing, precision, matching, skip rules.
Rules: no em dashes; companies only; no fact without a source URL; 1 request per 2 seconds; stop on 403 or 429. Commit on cloud/emkvr and push. Finish with: RESULT: <one sentence with the counts above, or BLOCKED and why>.
