# jx36_design_research [cloud]: research similar products, then two directions, then compare

Branch: `cloud/design_v2` (continue there; phase 2 stays on hold). Dayo's verdict on the phase 1 prototypes: not simple enough. He also asked at the start of the event for research into what similar products look like, and that research was never done properly. Do it now, first.

## Part 1: research (push by 02:15 UTC as docs/design_v2/RESEARCH.md)
Study how the products investors and accelerators actually use present (a) a list of companies and (b) one company in depth. Use the open web (marketing pages, public demo videos, screenshots in reviews, public ecosystem dashboards). For each product: 2 to 4 sentences on the list screen, 2 to 4 on the company screen, what they do well, what they do badly for a 50-year-old partner, and a link to what you looked at.
Products: Dealroom (including its public ecosystem dashboards, e.g. the Finnish startup ecosystem page), PitchBook, Crunchbase Pro, CB Insights, Tracxn, Specter, Harmonic, Affinity, Nordic 9, Vainu. Blue-specific and closest to our judges: Ocean Impact Navigator / 1000 Ocean Startups, Katapult Ocean's portfolio pages, the EU BlueInvest platform, Sifted's company profiles.
Finish with "Patterns": the 8 to 10 conventions these tools share (for example: dense sortable table with sticky header and filters on the left; company header with name, location, founded, stage, tags; tabbed sections; a timeline of dated events; every number with a source tooltip; saved lists). Note which conventions help a 40-60 partner and which hurt.

## Part 2: two directions, static prototypes with real snapshot data (push by 03:15 UTC)
Direction A, "Industry pattern": built from the Patterns list. docs/design_v2/a_home.html and a_company.html.
Direction B, "Dayo's": the home page is a country tab bar (Finland, Estonia, Sweden ...) and under it, immediately, a plain list of startups already on screen, no headline, no hero, no filters shown by default: name, town, what they do in one line, their newest public signal with date, and the verdict pill. Pick one and the company page goes in depth on everything we hold, section by section, with the source shown beside EVERY datum (a small source link or the source name in grey). The AI box ("What the public record says") sits near the top. docs/design_v2/b_home.html and b_company.html.
Both: same tokens.css, both themes, screenshot at 1440x900 and 390x844, read the PNGs, one self-critique round each. Keep it SIMPLE: if a 50-year-old partner cannot tell what to do in 5 seconds, cut.

## Part 3: compare (docs/design_v2/COMPARE.md)
A table: A versus B on the judges' criteria (clear in 5 seconds, on-brand, usable by 40-60, actionable, scalable to other countries and sectors, visual appeal) plus build cost before the Sat 18:30 UTC freeze. End with one recommendation and the reason. Dayo decides.

Rules as before: real data only, companies only, no partner logos, vendored fonts, no app templates touched in this task.
